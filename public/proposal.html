Luz de LunaMoonlight

A business coach in your pocket — for women rebuilding their lives.

Luz de Luna helps women and girls at risk of, or exiting, commercial sexual exploitation become independent entrepreneurs. It is the first solution to pair livelihood training and psychosocial support in a single coach — available 24/7, on any phone, without shame or judgment.

The problem

Two barriers, faced at once

Women trapped in or at risk of commercial sexual exploitation face two obstacles at the same time: no viable path to income, and the trauma that makes rebuilding feel impossible. Most programs address one or the other. And few reach women where they actually are — on the phone in their hand, at any hour they need it.

How it works

Meet Sol

Sol is a coach women reach through WhatsApp or Telegram. She meets each woman with a few simple questions, listens, and helps her take a first concrete step this week — turning what she already knows into real income.

“A specific person to message today, an offer with a price, and a page to share.”

The goal isn’t a side hustle that buys groceries. It’s a real income — the kind that pays.

Why it works — and how fast

Income that arrives quickly

For a woman leaving exploitation, speed is everything: a path that takes a year asks her to survive the exact gap that traps her. Sol is built for a first sale in the first weeks — then a steady ramp to an income she can truly live on.

The pilot & the coalition

Starting in Colombia, in 2026

Our first cohort — roughly 25 women in Bucaramanga, Colombia — launches in 2026 with in-person onboarding and about six months of digital follow-through — the window in which women ramp to a livable income. Luz de Luna is a social enterprise under Madiro, free for every participant and sustained through grants and enterprise partnerships.
